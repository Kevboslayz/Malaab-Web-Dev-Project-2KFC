The house rule

Confirmed never exceeds the number needed. Full means a queue in arrival order. A drop-out promotes number one automatically.

Fixed, and yours

Fixed
The eight surfaces, the four data states, the three viewer variants, and the house rule. These carry the marks.
A direction, not a cage
Malaab is a complete identity, and it is dark on purpose. Implement it as given and you will do well. Re-skin it if you would rather — including going light — but then every surface changes and the contrast has to hold just as well. Expect to be asked why.
Yours regardless
Component boundaries, props, where state lives, routing. Most of the marks are there.
