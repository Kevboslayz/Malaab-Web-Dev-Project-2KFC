The roster

The signature component of Malaab and the only place your house rule is visible: confirmed players never exceed the number needed; once full, people queue in arrival order; a cancellation promotes number one automatically and everybody shifts up. Six of your eight surfaces contain it. Build it before you build a screen.

Note · What the component is given A game and the viewer. Spots remaining is computed from confirmed signups, never stored. A queue position is the index in an ordered list, never a number you write into a row.

Note · This pack is dark on purpose The game is at night, under floodlights. If you re-skin it, a light version is fine — but then every surface goes light, and the contrast has to hold just as well. Half-dark is the one outcome that will cost you marks.
